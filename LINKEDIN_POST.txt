The coffee that sells the most cups isn't necessarily the one that brings in the most revenue. ☕

That was one of my takeaways from Analyst Club's Friday Challenge, where I built a Coffee Sales Insights dashboard in Tableau.

I explored 3,636 coffee vending-machine sales across eight drinks, asking: What should stay stocked? When should the machine be ready for peak demand? And what do the monthly trends actually tell us?

Three findings stood out:
• Latte led revenue at 27,866.30, while Americano with Milk led volume with 824 cups.
• 10–11 a.m. was the busiest hour, with 349 cups sold across the dataset.
• October 2024 generated the highest monthly revenue, at 13,891.16.

My business takeaway: prioritise availability of the leading drinks, plan replenishment ahead of demand peaks, and investigate the drivers of strong months before building forecasts around them.

I also added a time-slot revenue view to explore how sales change through the day. March 2025 is only a partial month, so comparisons need that context. Revenue figures use the dataset's unspecified local currency.

This project helped me practise translating dashboard patterns into business questions, practical recommendations and clearly defined KPIs.

Thank you, @Analyst Club, for the challenge and the opportunity to keep learning through practice!
